# Garage Pro demo video: script and storyboard

Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, about 80 seconds. Calm, confident voice. Screens come from the Garage Pro design canvas.
Motion style: slow push-ins on the part being described, a soft green highlight ring, short on-screen captions, and quick slide transitions between screens.

| # | Time | Screen | On screen (motion) | Voice-over |
|---|------|--------|--------------------|------------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | Title card | Logo mark scales in, tagline types on | Garage Pro. The workshop system built for UAE garages. |
| 2 | 0:05–0:15 | Service intake | Push in on the plate search, then the damage diagram and signature | A customer pulls in. Search the plate, and their history is already there. Note the complaint, mark any damage, and take a signature in under two minutes. |
| 3 | 0:15–0:25 | Estimation | Three option cards slide up, then the WhatsApp preview pops in | Garage Pro builds three estimate options from your price list and sends them on WhatsApp. The customer approves from their phone. |
| 4 | 0:25–0:36 | Job card, then dashboard bays | Pan down the job card sections, cut to the live bay board | The approved estimate becomes a job card. Parts are reserved from stock, labour goes to a technician, and every bay is tracked live. |
| 5 | 0:36–0:45 | Inventory, then purchases | Highlight the low-stock rows, then the three-way match turning green | When stock runs low, Garage Pro suggests the order, matches the supplier invoice, and records your input VAT. |
| 6 | 0:45–0:55 | Billing job card, then A4 invoice | Totals count up, the A4 invoice drops in like a printed page | At delivery, issue a numbered tax invoice in one click. It is locked, VAT-correct, and ready to print. |
| 7 | 0:55–1:05 | E-invoicing and Wafeq | The invoice travels across the five stages, statuses tick to green | For business customers, the e-invoice goes out through Wafeq in the PINT AE format, built for the UAE e-invoicing rollout. |
| 8 | 1:05–1:13 | Dashboard | Wide shot, KPI numbers count up, chart bars grow | And every morning, one dashboard shows revenue, open jobs, customer dues and stock. |
| 9 | 1:13–1:20 | End card | Logo, call to action | Garage Pro. Book your demo at [YOUR WEBSITE]. |

Word count: about 190 words, which fits 80 seconds at a relaxed pace.

Notes

- Replace [YOUR WEBSITE] before recording.
